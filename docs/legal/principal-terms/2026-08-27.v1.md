## Read this first

Plain English is a requirement, not a style choice. Consent under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act
2023** must be *informed*, and text you cannot read is not informed consent.

**Two things are worth knowing before anything else:**

1. **Amana is not a bank.** Your money is held in an account provided by our licensed banking
   partner, not by us. We move it and control it; we do not hold it.
2. **You will be able to see how the people you give money to are spending it** — including, on
   many payments, *where* they were. That is the product. Section 5 says exactly what you see, and
   the people you give money to are told the same thing in their own terms.

## 1. Who we are

Amana operates a controlled-spending wallet. We are the **data controller** for the information
described here.

Contact for anything in this notice, including all the rights in section 7:
**[SUPPORT CHANNEL — TO BE FILLED IN BEFORE LAUNCH]**.

## 2. What you are signing up for

You fund one wallet and issue **sub-wallets** to people you trust — a child, a member of staff, a
rider. For each, you set what they may spend, on what, and when. They spend within those rules
without asking you each time; when they need something outside the rules, they ask, and you decide.

## 3. What we collect from you, and why

| What | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Your phone number | To sign you in, and to reach you | Sign-in is by SMS code |
| Your **BVN** and **NIN** | Required by CBN rules before a wallet can hold money | **Encrypted at rest.** Not shown to anyone you pair with |
| Your wallet activity | To run the wallet, apply your rules, and show you what happened | |

**On BVN and NIN specifically:** these are collected because Nigerian regulation requires identity
verification before an account can hold funds, and they set the limits on what your wallet can hold.
They are stored encrypted and are never shown to your agents, to vendors, or on any public page.

## 4. What it costs

- **₦100 for each payment out** to someone else. Your first **five payments are free, for the life
  of the account** — not five a month.
- **Adding money is free**, and so is moving money between your own wallets.
- If you pay someone more than ₦10,000, the government's **₦50 electronic transfer levy** applies.
  We collect it and pass it on; we do not keep it.
- Buying airtime, data or paying a bill through Amana is priced at the time you buy it.

We do not charge a subscription.

## 5. What you can see about the people you fund — and what they are told

When someone spends from a sub-wallet you fund, you can see:

- how much, to whom, and when
- what kind of purchase it was
- **where they were when they spent**, on payments where their phone provided a location
- any photo or note they attached

**They are told this.** Their own terms state plainly that you can see all of the above, including
their location. We do not offer, and will not build, a way to watch someone who has not been told.

**This is a responsibility, not just a feature.** If you are funding a member of staff, employment
law and basic fairness both apply to what you do with it. If you are funding a child, so does your
judgement.

## 6. What we do with your information

- **We do not sell it.**
- **We do not share your spending with other users**, beyond what section 5 describes within your own
  household.
- We share what we must with our banking partner to move money, and with the authorities when the
  law requires it.
- We use it to run and improve Amana, including detecting fraud and misuse.

## 7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may, at any time: **see** what we hold about you,
**correct** anything wrong, **ask us to delete** your account, **object** to particular uses, and
**complain** to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

**How, today: contact support** at the channel in section 1. We will action it and record that we
did.

Some information we must keep even after you close your account — records of money moved are
required by financial regulation, and we keep them for as long as the law requires.

## 8. Ending it

You can stop using Amana at any time and ask us to close your account. Withdraw your balance first;
we cannot return money that has already been sent to someone else.

We may suspend an account we believe is being used for fraud or against these terms. If we do, we
will tell you why unless the law stops us.

## 9. Changing this text

Your agreement is recorded against **the version you were actually shown**. If we change what this
says in a way that matters, we publish a new version and ask you again. **Agreeing to v1 is not
agreeing to v2.**

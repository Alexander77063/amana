# Amana — what you should know before you use this wallet

**Version `2026-08-27.v1`** · Shown to an **agent** — someone spending from a wallet another person
funds. A child, a member of staff, a rider.

> ⚠️ **NOT YET LEGALLY REVIEWED**, and this one needs a lawyer more than the others. Two things make
> it harder than an ordinary notice, and both are flagged in place below: many agents are **children**,
> who cannot legally agree to this themselves, and many are **staff**, whose agreement to an employer
> is not freely given in the way the law means. See "A note for the lawyer" at the end.

---

## The most important part, first

**The person who funds this wallet can see how you spend it — including where you were.**

Every time you pay someone from this wallet, they can see:

- how much you spent, who you paid, and when
- what kind of purchase it was
- **where you were when you paid**, when your phone provides a location
- any photo or note you added

This is not hidden and it is not optional — it is how the wallet works. **You are being told
because you have a right to know**, not because we need your permission to do it.

If you are not comfortable with that, do not use the wallet. Nothing else on your phone is affected,
and nobody can see anything you do outside Amana.

---

## What this wallet is

Someone — a parent, an employer — has given you money to spend, with rules attached. The rules
might be an amount, the kinds of things you can buy, or the hours you can buy them in.

**What this means for you day to day:**

- You do not have to ask permission for every purchase. Inside the rules, you just pay.
- If something is outside the rules, the payment is refused and you are told **why** — not just
  "declined". You can ask the person who funds it, from inside the app.
- **It is not your money.** You are spending someone else's, under their rules, and they can change
  or stop it.

## What we collect from you

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your phone number | To sign you in, by SMS code |
| Your **NIN** | Required by Nigerian rules before a wallet can hold money |
| Where you were when you paid | Shown to the person funding the wallet, as above |
| Photos or notes you attach | Shown to the same person |

Your NIN is **encrypted** and is never shown to the person funding your wallet, to shops, or on any
public page. **We do not ask you for your BVN.**

## What we will not do

- **We do not track you when you are not paying.** Location is recorded at the moment of a payment,
  not in between.
- **We do not sell your information.**
- **We do not show your spending to anyone except the person funding that wallet.**
- **We do not let them see anything from before you were paired with them.**

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may, at any time: **see** what we hold about you,
**correct** anything wrong, **object** to how we use it, and **complain** to the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission.

**How, today: contact support** at **[SUPPORT CHANNEL — TO BE FILLED IN BEFORE LAUNCH]**.

**If you want to stop:** tell the person funding the wallet, or tell us. You can stop using the
wallet whenever you want. What you have already spent cannot be unsent, and the record of it stays
with the person who funded it.

## If you are under 18

You should read this **with a parent or guardian**, and they need to agree to it as well as you. If
you are being asked to use this wallet by someone who is not your parent or guardian, tell your
parent or guardian first.

## Changing this text

Your agreement is recorded against **the version you were actually shown**. If we change what this
says in a way that matters, we publish a new version and ask again.

## A note for the lawyer — not shown to agents

Three things about this document need a decision that engineering cannot make:

1. **Children.** Under NDPA 2023 a child cannot give valid consent; a parent or guardian must give
   it for them. The product's core case is a parent issuing a sub-wallet to their child, so this is
   the **normal** path, not an edge case. `user_consents.granted_by_user_id` exists to record a
   guardian accepting on an agent's behalf, and **is not yet wired to any flow** — deliberately,
   because who declares a minor, and when, is a product decision. See the go-live checklist §8b.

2. **Staff, and freely-given consent.** An employee's agreement to their employer is not *freely
   given* in the sense the law means. For staff agents, consent is likely the wrong lawful basis
   entirely — contract or legitimate interest is more defensible — which means this single document
   may need to describe **two different bases for two populations**, or be split in two.

3. **The monitoring disclosure is notice, not consent.** Location capture is not optional and the
   product does not function without it, so asking for consent we would not honour a refusal of
   would be misleading. It is written as *"you are being told because you have a right to know"*.
   Confirm that framing is right, and that the legitimate-interest assessment behind it is recorded.

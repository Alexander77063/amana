# Amana — user acquisition plan

**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Related:** [`PRICING.md`](../business/PRICING.md),
[`embedded-distribution-strategy.md`](../business/embedded-distribution-strategy.md)

That distribution note is about **monetising an audience once it exists**, and says so plainly:
"distribution is a scale lever, not a launch lever." This document is the missing half — how the
audience comes to exist in the first place.

## What we are actually acquiring

Not a user. A **household**, and every household is two sign-ups that must both land:

- a **principal** who has money and a control problem
- at least one **agent** who has to be persuaded to spend through it

A principal who signs up alone is worth nothing — no sub-wallet, no spend, no fee. **The activation
event is the first agent spend, not the principal's registration.** Everything below optimises for
that, and any metric that counts principals alone is measuring the wrong thing.

## Who the first hundred are

Concretely, not as personas:

1. **Parents of secondary-school and university students** paying for transport, food and school
   costs remotely. Highest pain, clearest control story, and the agent is captive — the money comes
   through the parent regardless, so Amana changes the mechanism, not the relationship.
2. **Small business owners with riders or shop staff** who currently hand over cash or make ad-hoc
   transfers, and reconcile nothing. Higher spend frequency, so better unit economics per household.
3. **Adult children supporting parents** — the same primitive inverted. Lower frequency, but the
   trust story is strongest and word of mouth is unusually reliable.

Start with **(1)**. Highest pain, clearest wedge, and it is the population the whole product was
designed around.

## Channels, in the order they will be tried

**1. Founder-led, hand-recruited (stages 2–3).** Ten, then fifty households recruited personally.
Not a growth channel — a learning channel. The goal is the pairing conversation: watching a parent
explain Amana to their child is where the onboarding copy gets written.

**2. School-gate and campus clusters.** Households cluster. One parent in a WhatsApp class group who
solves the "what did you do with the money I sent you" problem is worth more than any ad, because
the objection ("is my money safe with an app I have not heard of?") is answered by a person the
listener already trusts.

**3. The retailer side as an acquisition channel.** This is the asymmetry worth exploiting and the
reason the marketplace matters commercially before it matters financially. A retailer onboarded to
Amana has a standing reason to tell customers about it — they get paid on redemption. Retailers
acquire buyers; buyers make retailers worth joining. **Building the supply side first is an
acquisition decision, not only a product one.**

**4. Payroll and cooperative embedding.** Employers and cooperatives already move money to the same
people every month. This is the highest-volume channel and the slowest to close; start conversations
early, expect nothing before stage 4.

**Not doing:** paid social, influencer, or anything that acquires a principal without an agent. It
buys the wrong half of the household.

## What acquisition may cost

From [`PRICING.md`](../business/PRICING.md): roughly **₦24.4M/yr profit at ~1,000 households**, so
about **₦24,000 per household per year**, before the VAS line.

That allows meaningful CAC — but only against **activated** households. A principal who never issues
a sub-wallet costs the free-spend grant and returns nothing, so any paid channel must be measured on
first-agent-spend, never on registration.

**The first five spends are free, lifetime.** That is the acquisition budget, and it is already
spent by design: about ₦500 of forgone fee per household, deliberately placed at the point of
maximum doubt rather than handed over as a discount.

## The metric

**Activated households per week**, where activated = at least one agent spend through a sub-wallet.

Everything else — registrations, downloads, wallet funding — is a leading indicator that can move
while the real number stays flat, and has been known to.
